## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Etihad Plaza CPA**, your in-room entertainment and information hub at Etihad Plaza. This app runs on the television in your apartment and gives you quick access to:

- Health and safety information
- Hotel messages and announcements
- Hypermarket catalogues
- EY Plaza news, gallery, and room information
- On-site facilities and contact details
- Live TV channels (general and Etihad-branded)
- Restaurant menus and dining options

Everything is designed for use with your **TV remote control** — no mouse, keyboard, or touch screen is required.

---

## 2. Before You Begin

### What you need

- The in-room television powered on
- The TV remote control (D-pad with directional arrows and OK/Select button)
- The TV set to the correct HDMI input (if applicable — usually configured by hotel staff)

### How the app works

- The app opens automatically when you turn on the TV (or select the hotel entertainment input).
- All screens share a consistent **Etihad-branded** look with a gold and dark colour scheme.
- The **Welcome Screen** is your home base. Every section returns here when you press **Back**.
- Content (menus, messages, channels, facilities) is loaded from the hotel CMS and updated remotely — you do not need to configure anything.

---

## 3. Using Your Remote Control

The app is built for Android TV remotes. The buttons below are used throughout the guide.

| Remote button | What it does |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Up ▲** | Move focus up (previous item, previous row) |
| **Down ▼** | Move focus down (next item, next row) |
| **Left ◀** | Move focus left |
| **Right ▶** | Move focus right |
| **OK / Select / Enter** | Confirm selection, open item, play/pause (in video viewers) |
| **Back** | Go back one step — exit a viewer, leave a screen, or return to Welcome |
| **Menu** | On the Welcome Screen, works like OK to open the highlighted menu item |

### Focus indicator

When you move between items with the directional pad, the currently selected item is highlighted with a **gold border** or background. Always look for the highlighted item before pressing OK.

> **Tip:** From any feature screen, press **Back** once to return to the Welcome Screen. From the Welcome Screen, press **Back** again to exit the app.

## 5. Welcome Screen (Home)

The Welcome Screen is the main hub of the app. From here you can reach every feature.

### What you see

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Header** | Etihad logo, live clock, current date, and Abu Dhabi weather |
| **Welcome message** | Arabic “Hayyakum” (حياكم) greeting image plus a personalised welcome message |
| **Apartment number** | Your room/apartment number shown at the bottom of the screen |
| **Navigation bar** | Eight menu tiles for all app sections |

### Navigation menu

Use **Left** and **Right** on the remote to move between menu items. Seven items are visible at a time; arrow indicators appear when more items are off-screen. Press **OK** to open the highlighted section.

| Icon | Menu item | Opens |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Health | Occupational Health & Safety | Safety videos, policies, and emergency information |
| Messages | Messages | Hotel announcements and notifications |
| Cart | Hypermarket | Store catalogues and shopping information |
| Plaza | EY Plaza | Plaza news, gallery, and room details |
| Facilities | Etihad Facilities | On-site facilities and contact numbers |
| Channel | Etihad Channel | Etihad-branded live TV channels |
| TV | TV Channel | General live TV channels |
| Dining | Dining | Restaurant menus and venue information |

### Messages badge

If you have unread messages, a **badge** appears on the Messages menu icon showing the number of unseen notifications.

## 14. Emergency Alerts

The app may display **full-screen emergency alerts** at any time when hotel management sends an important announcement to your room or building.

### Alert severity levels

| Level | Appearance | Meaning |
|-------|------------|---------|
| **Info** | Blue styling | General information |
| **Warning** | Amber/orange styling | Important notice — read carefully |
| **Critical** | Red styling | Urgent — immediate attention required |

### What you see

- Alert title and message
- Optional call-to-action button (if provided)
- **Dismiss** button

### How to respond

1. **Read the alert carefully.**
2. Press **OK** or **Back** to dismiss the alert, or highlight **Dismiss** and press **OK**.
3. Once dismissed, the same alert will **not appear again** on your TV.

## 15. App Updates

Occasionally the app checks for software updates and prompts you to install a newer version.

### Update dialog

When an update is available, a dialog shows:

- New version number and build
- Release notes (what changed)
- **Update Now** button
- **Later** button (if the update is optional)

### How to update

1. When the update dialog appears, use **Left / Right** to choose **Update Now** or **Later**.
2. Press **OK** on **Update Now** to download and install.
3. A progress bar shows the download status.
4. When complete, follow the on-screen instructions to install the update.
5. The TV may restart or relaunch the app after installation.

## 16. Returning Home & Exiting

### Navigation summary

```
Any feature screen  ──Back──▶  Welcome Screen (Home)  ──Back──▶  Exit app
        │
        └── Full-screen viewer  ──Back──▶  Parent screen  ──Back──▶  Welcome Screen
```

| Where you are | Press Back once | Press Back twice |
|---------------|-----------------|------------------|
| PDF / image / video viewer | Return to parent screen | — |
| Feature screen (Dining, TV, etc.) | Return to Welcome Screen | — |
| Welcome Screen | Exit the app | — |

### Quick reference — getting home from anywhere

1. If you are in a full-screen viewer (video, PDF, image, gallery), press **Back** to close it.
2. Press **Back** again to return to the Welcome Screen.
3. From Welcome, press **Back** to exit the app.

## 17. Troubleshooting

### App will not start

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Black screen | Turn the TV off and on; wait 30 seconds |
| Stuck on splash | Press any remote key to skip; if still stuck, restart the TV |
| Wrong input | Ask reception to confirm the correct HDMI/source input |

### Content not loading

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| "Loading…" for a long time | Wait up to 30 seconds; press **Back** and re-enter the section |
| Error with **TRY AGAIN** | Highlight **TRY AGAIN** and press **OK** |
| Empty list (no channels, no messages) | Content may not be assigned to your room yet — contact reception |
| Outdated menu or facilities | Content is managed by hotel staff; report to reception |

### TV channels not playing

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| Black player / no video | Select a different channel, then return to your chosen channel |
| Buffering | Wait a few seconds; check with reception if all channels are affected |
| Audio but no picture | Press **Back**, re-enter the channel, and try full screen again |

### Messages not appearing

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| No new messages | Switch filter to **All**; messages may already be marked seen |
| Badge count wrong | Open Messages and read unread items; badge updates automatically |
| Attachment won't open | Try again; PDF and large files may take a moment to load |

### Remote not responding

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| No movement on screen | Replace or recharge remote batteries |
| Buttons work on TV but not in app | Exit and reopen the app; restart the TV if needed |
| Double-back exits too fast | Press **Back** once and wait briefly before pressing again |

## 18. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need an account or password?**  
A: No. The app recognises your room automatically via the in-room TV device.

**Q: Can I change the language?**  
A: The app displays content as configured by hotel management. The welcome greeting includes Arabic “Hayyakum”; other text follows CMS settings.

**Q: Why does my welcome message say “Guest”?**  
A: Your guest profile may not be linked yet. Reception can verify your room assignment.

**Q: Will I miss messages if the TV is off?**  
A: Messages are stored on the server and appear when you open the Messages section. Unread items show a badge on the Welcome Screen.

**Q: Can I order food from the Dining screen?**  
A: The Dining screen is for menu browsing. Contact the restaurant or reception to place orders.

**Q: What is the difference between Etihad Channel and TV Channel?**  
A: **Etihad Channel** carries Etihad-branded and dedicated packages. **TV Channel** provides the general IPTV channel lineup.

**Q: How do I report a problem with the TV or app?**  
A: Contact hotel reception. Mention your apartment number shown on the Welcome Screen.

**Q: Is there a settings or preferences menu?**  
A: No. The app is pre-configured for your room. All content is managed remotely by hotel staff.
